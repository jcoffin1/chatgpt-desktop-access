# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in ChatGPT Desktop Access to be welcoming, respectful,
and accessible. Everyone may contribute regardless of disability, experience,
identity, background, or communication style.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, constructive, and respectful.
- Describe technical and accessibility problems without attacking people.
- Respect different assistive-technology workflows and user preferences.
- Protect privacy. Do not repost private conversations, logs, account details,
  credentials, or personal information.
- Accept reasonable moderation and correction when a discussion becomes harmful
  or moves away from the project's purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks,
sexualized content, unwanted disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces representing this project. Repository maintainers may edit or remove
content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct or privacy concerns through the repository owner's private
GitHub contact options. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical.
